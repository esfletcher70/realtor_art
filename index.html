How it works

Three steps for you. A gift for them.

You stay the hero at closing. We take care of the art, the frame, and the drive.

  1. 1

    Send a photo

    Text or email a picture of the front of the home, plus the address, the family's name, and their move-in year.

  2. 2

    Approve the proof

    Within 48 hours you get a digital proof. Ask for changes, or approve it. If you don't love it, you get a full refund.

  3. 3

    We frame & deliver

    We print it on archival paper, mat and frame it, add your logo and a handwritten card, then drop it at your office or the client's door.

How each portrait is made

Made with AI. Checked by a person.

We use AI image tools to paint each portrait, so you get it in days, not weeks, at a price that works as a closing gift. Then a person checks every detail against your photo before you see a proof.

  • True to the house

    Window counts, rooflines, trim colors, and the tree out front are checked against your photo and fixed by hand when needed.

  • Real watercolor feel

    Soft washes, ink linework, and paper texture, printed on archival fine-art paper.

  • Sharp at 16×20

    Every portrait is upscaled and print-checked so it stays crisp at full frame size.

Questions

Good to know

What photo should I send?

A straight-on photo of the front of the home in daylight. A phone photo is fine. Please send a photo you or your client took. Listing photos usually belong to the photographer, so only send one if the photographer has said it's OK.

Is it really made with AI?

Yes, and we say so openly. AI tools paint the watercolor, and a person checks and corrects the details before you see a proof. That's how we keep the price and turnaround closing-gift friendly.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us on the proof and we'll fix it. If you still don't love it, you get a full refund before anything is printed.

Where do you deliver?

We hand-deliver free across Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties, to your office or straight to your client. Anywhere else in the U.S. ships for a flat fee.

How fast can I get it?

Proofs arrive within 48 hours. Framed pieces are usually delivered 5–7 days after you approve the proof. Need it sooner? Ask, and we'll do our best.

Can you add my branding?

Yes. Your logo goes on the label on the back, and we include a handwritten card in your words. Send them once and we'll use them on every gift.
